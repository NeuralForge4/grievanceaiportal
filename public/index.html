HOW IT WORKS

From Complaint to Action

GrievanceAI uses a hybrid approach: machine learning for sentiment analysis and rule-based logic for urgency, to help officers prioritise what matters most.

1. Submit

Citizen files a complaint with relevant details.

2. Analyse

AI classifies sentiment and checks urgency rules.

3. Prioritise

Complaint is sorted into P1 to P4 priority tiers.

4. Review

Authorised officer makes the final decision.

About & Responsible AI

How priority is decided

  • P1: safety risk such as live wires, fire, gas leak, flooding
  • P2: repeated unresolved complaint, critical timeline, or case older than 30 days
  • P3: standard complaint
  • P4: routine status check

Sentiment is scored separately. A negative tone is not an emergency, and a calm complaint can still be P1.

Safeguards

  • Officers make the final decision
  • Contact details are visible only to authorised officers assigned to that department; administrators can view all departments
  • Dashboard and analytics need sign-in
  • Training data must be licensed, sanitised and de-duplicated
